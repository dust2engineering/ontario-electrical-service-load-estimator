Ontario Electrical Service Load Estimator

Rough-size the electrical service for a small commercial unit from its connected loads — planning estimate with stated demand factors.

Result

Your service estimate will appear here.

Planning demand factors used here: lighting 100%, kitchen equipment 80%, HVAC 100%, receptacles 50%, other 100%. They are stated openly so the estimate can be argued with — the number that goes on a permit application comes from the Canadian Electrical Code Section 8 calculation by the electrical designer. This tool answers the earlier question: roughly how big a service does this unit need, and is the existing 200 A panel going to survive the new kitchen?
