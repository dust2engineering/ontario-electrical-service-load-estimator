# Example 1 — Restaurant unit, 120/208 V 3-phase

Connected: lighting 6 kW, kitchen equipment 45 kW, HVAC 18 kW, receptacles 8 kW, other 5 kW = 82 kW.
Demand: 6 x 1.0 + 45 x 0.8 + 18 x 1.0 + 8 x 0.5 + 5 x 1.0 = 69.0 kVA.
Amps = 69,000 / (1.732 x 208) = 192 A -> a 200 A service is the planning answer; the CEC Section 8
calculation decides the final design.
